## 4. The "why" pattern behind the strongest sellers

Products sell for a few repeating reasons. Each candidate should be tagged with the reasons that apply:

1. **Repeat purchase or consumable** (beauty, supplements, cleaning refills)
2. **Visible in a 10-second video** (gadgets, beauty tools, transformations)
3. **Under about $25, an impulse price**
4. **Giftable or seasonal** (jewelry, decor, Q4)
5. **Solves a specific annoyance** (pet hair, cleaning, organising)
6. **Collectability or nostalgia** (cards, blind boxes)

Our supply limits us mostly to reasons 2, 3, 4 and 5. Margin pushes toward bundles priced $25–40, which sits awkwardly with reason 3.

## 5. Cross-check: margin-viable vs popular [data]

The 251 margin-viable CJ products have a median of **418** stores listing them, against **49** for the whole US catalogue. 106 of them are in the top 15% by popularity. That is either validated demand or a crowded field. **eBay sold counts (Terapeak) decide which**, and so does the number of competing eBay listings.
