## Macro

- **Q3 2026:** eBay GMV $23.2B (+15%). **US GMV $12.5B (+27%, about +22% excluding acquisitions)** (P).
- **Q4 2026 guidance:** GMV $23.7–24.1B (P). Q4 is about 27% of annual GMV (our calculation).
- **Census Q2 2026:** US e-commerce up 12.2% Y/Y; 17.1% of retail (P).
- **Adobe holiday 2025:** online sales $257.8B (+6.8%), Cyber Week $44.2B (S).

## Where eBay beats Amazon and Temu (qualitative)

- Fitment and hard-to-find parts
- Collectibles, helped by authentication (Authenticity Guarantee for cards above ~$200)
- **Not** commodity electronics, where Temu wins on price

## Gaps

- No public ASP or sell-through by category. Terapeak in Seller Hub shows sell-through for windows of 90 days or less. We need it, or the Browse API.
