# 31 — What the top-performing Shopify stores have in common

> 2026-11-04. Phase 4 of `docs/plan/top-stores-inspiration-plan.md`. Study set: **17 stores** with independent proof of performance (4 grade A, 13 grade B; doc 30), from 30,365 Shopify stores found in the Tranco top 1M. Store names and per-store dossiers are private (`data/private/research/study17-dossiers.md`, also in the owner's Claude storage vault). Counts below come from our measured data (`[data]`, homepage code and product feeds) and dated research; ranks are popularity (`[proxy]`), not sales. Counts checked by the advisor against the data file.

## Who they are

- 15 own-brand makers, 1 reseller, 1 manufacturer our rules mislabelled as dropship. **None is a zero-inventory store**: their success shows demand and method, not something we can copy outright.
- All price in USD; 13 US-based, 4 based in Hong Kong or Canada that ship to 33 to 96 countries.
- 2 show distress (a 2026 bankruptcy, an earlier creditor takeover): demand and method evidence only.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Count | Cheap for a small store? |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads tag | 15 of 17 | yes, at small budgets |
| Meta pixel / TikTok pixel | 14 / 12 of 17 | yes |
| Klaviyo email on the homepage | 13 of 17 | **yes, highest-value cheap tool** |
| SMS marketing | 5 of 17 | yes, after email |
| Reviews app with counts per product | 11 of 17 | **yes** |
| Buy now, pay later | 10 of 17 | yes (platform setting) |
| Affiliate programme | 4 of 17 | yes |
| Hero product plus an ecosystem (accessories, consumables, memberships) | 6 of 17 | partly: consumables and add-ons yes, own hardware no |
| Median entry price at or below USD 45 | 9 of 17 | fits impulse buying |
| Discount over 60% of the range | 4 of 17 (mostly apparel) | a warning, not a model: list prices are not real prices |
| Growth by acquisitions or licensing | 5 of 17 | no |
| Rank rising over the last 9+ months | 12 of 17 (the 4 falling are large legacy brands) | n/a |

## What a small zero-inventory store can borrow

1. **Email first:** a Klaviyo welcome and abandoned-cart flow is the most common retention tool among winners.
2. **Proof on every product:** review counts and stars on each product card; dated claims; one credible badge (award, certification).
3. **A hero product with add-ons:** pick one product that can carry accessories or consumables, and sell the add-ons on every order.
4. **Seasonal and partner drops on a calendar** rather than a constant sale.
5. **Paid social plus search:** Meta and Google together are standard; TikTok is close behind.
6. **Free-shipping threshold, bundles, BNPL:** cheap conversion levers almost all of them use.

## What needs scale or rights (do not plan on it)

League and brand licensing, acquisitions, own patented hardware with apps, five-channel paid acquisition, exclusive distribution, and catalogues above 1,000 products with back-office tooling.

## Gaps

Revenue is known for one store only (filings); the rest are estimates or company claims. Ad spend and which ads run are unknown until the owner's manual ad-library check. Categories in our rules mislabel a few stores; the dossiers use the researchers' descriptions.
