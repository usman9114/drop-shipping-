## Category depth

There's no hard data. Sources point toward electronics accessories, home, fitness, fashion accessories, pet, beauty and home office. CJ's "Top 25 trending in USA 2025" list is marketing without prices or stock levels.

## Implications

- **Variant-level stock sync is mandatory.** Out-of-stock cancellations count as eBay defects (03).
- Prefer **single-variant or few-variant** products for the PoC.
