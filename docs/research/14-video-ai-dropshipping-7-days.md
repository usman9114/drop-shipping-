# 14 — External source review: "I tried AI dropshipping for 7 days" (YouTube transcript)

> Reviewed 2026-11-03 by the orchestrator. Source: a transcript the owner uploaded (Mark Tilbury channel). **[secondary, promotional]**: the video sends viewers to an affiliate "top link in description" for Build Your Store and AutoDS.

## What the video did

- Built a Shopify store with Build Your Store AI (sports and fitness niche, chosen by gut feel).
- Imported AutoDS "winning products": breathing trainer, golf club clips, resistance band, beanie.
- Promoted one product through **one micro-influencer story** (~14.5k Instagram followers, $50).
- Result in 24 hours: 10 real sales at £13.42 → $170.85 revenue.

- "Scale to 20 products = thousands per month" was **not tested**: the same audience didn't buy the other three products.

## Red flags

- **Affiliate and promotional framing.** The opening montage of "millions/month" claims comes from other people.
- **".store gets 87% more traffic and ranks 2× higher"** traces to the .store registry's own marketing. Not independent.
- **Health claim in the ad** ("increases your red blood cell count") with no visible #ad disclosure. That's an **FTC endorsement-guide and health-claim risk**.
- **AutoDS "trending products"** are the same list every subscriber sees, which is the saturation problem (02 §4). AutoDS mostly sources from AliExpress and Amazon, which **breaks eBay's dropshipping policy** if used there (01 §1). It's fine on Shopify.

## What's useful to us

1. **Test orders before promoting.** He ordered samples first. This matches our Phase 1 test orders.
2. **Creator traffic as a cheap demand probe.** $50 bought 10 sales for an impulse-priced item. A possible **later channel test** (a Shopify store plus micro-creators) once eBay data exists. Not a substitute for eBay's built-in traffic.
3. **Pricing:** he used 2–2.5× cost on Shopify (no marketplace fee). On eBay our model needs about **3× landed cost** to pass (13 §ceilings / `scout.margin`), which confirms our stricter rule.
4. **Niche picked by gut feel.** Our process (Trends + eBay API + risk + margin) is the evidence-based version of the same step.

## Plan impact

**No change to the niche process.** Added to `compliance-officer`: FTC endorsement disclosure and health or performance claims, for any future creator promotion.
