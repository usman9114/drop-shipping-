# 05 — Legal/IP/compliance risks and other platforms

> Researched 2026-11-03 by a web-search agent. **Informational, not legal advice.** Most sources are secondary: law-firm blogs, seller tools and forums. Several primary texts did not load: Amazon G201808410, EUR-Lex GPSR, the FTC Mail Order Rule page. Points marked **[unverified]** rest only on secondary sources.

## 1. Copyright: the "clone the listing" part

- **Copying a competitor's or supplier's photos or text without permission is generally infringement.** Removing a watermark doesn't fix that.
- eBay's policy says listing content "shouldn't be copied from any websites, third-party catalogs or other sources unless you have approval".
- Rights owners file reports through **VeRO**. Upheld reports get listings removed. Repeat violations lead to restrictions and then suspension.
- **AliExpress and supplier images are not licensed to resellers by default [unverified].** Model photos carry extra risk because the models' image rights may not be cleared.
- **AI-editing someone else's photo still produces a derivative work [unverified for our exact case].** "Better images" must start from **our own photos**: a sample unit photographed ourselves, or AI scenes built around it. The other option is supplier images with **written permission**.
- So the clean version of the idea is **"find a good product, then make our own better listing"**, not "clone the listing".

## 2. Trademark, counterfeits, design patents

- Branded goods and "dupes" carry counterfeit risk. eBay removes them, and repeat offenses end accounts.
- **Design patents are the trap for trending gadgets.** "Schedule A" lawsuits in N.D. Illinois sue many anonymous marketplace sellers at once and freeze their payouts. Default judgments are common.
- Search USPTO for the product's design before listing any trending gadget. Amazon runs a Neutral Patent Evaluator program.

## 3. Product safety and compliance

| Regime | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| **US CPSC** | In July 2024 the CPSC ruled Amazon a "distributor" of hazardous third-party products. Its Online Sellers guide covers drop shippers. Avoid children's products: CPSIA requires a Children's Product Certificate and third-party testing. |
| **FCC** | Anything with radio (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, RF) needs equipment authorization before it is marketed. Avoid uncertified electronics. |
| **Prop 65 (California)** | The seller carries the warning-label burden. |
| **EU GPSR** (in force since 2024-12-13) | Each product needs an EU-based **responsible person**, shown on the listing. Marketplaces must remove non-compliant listings. That is a big barrier to selling China-direct into the EU. |
| **Lithium batteries** | Shipping them needs dangerous-goods documentation (UN 38.3). Avoid. |
| **Liability** | As seller of record, **we** are who the buyer, platform and regulator come after. |

## 4. Consumer protection

- **FTC Mail Order Rule:**
  - Ship within the time you advertise, or within 30 days if you state none.
  - Otherwise get the buyer's consent to the delay, or refund.
  - Delivery claims need a reasonable basis. Fashion Nova paid a $9.3M settlement in 2023.
  - **Listing "3-day handling" on a 20-day AliExpress item breaks this rule.**
- **EU 14-day withdrawal right:** buyers can return for any reason, and the refund includes outbound shipping. Directive (EU) 2023/2673 adds an online withdrawal button.

## 5. Tax and business setup

- **Marketplace facilitator laws:** eBay, Amazon and Walmart collect and remit US sales tax. Sales on our own Shopify store are our responsibility.
- **Resale certificates** stop suppliers from charging us sales tax.
- **1099-K threshold:** reported back at over $20k and over 200 transactions for 2025–26 (One Big Beautiful Bill Act). Sources conflict, so check with the IRS.
- Most platforms want a registered business, a tax ID and a business bank account.

## 6. Platform comparison

| Platform | Dropshipping stance | Constraints | Friendliness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **eBay** | Allowed **from wholesale suppliers only** | Retail arbitrage banned; tracking and handling-time standards (see 01) | Medium |
| **Shopify** (own store) | Allowed | You pay for traffic (ads); payment-processor risk | High, but traffic is expensive |
| **Walmart Marketplace** | Allowed from legitimate suppliers | Retail-sourced banned; real tracking; payouts held up to 14 days for new sellers | Medium |
| **Amazon** | Only as **seller of record** | Your name on packing slips and invoices; no Amazon-to-Amazon; hard to get approved | Medium-low |
| **Allegro (PL)** | Tolerated | Accurate dispatch times | Medium **[unverified]** |
| **OnBuy (UK)** | Tolerated | Seller of record | Medium-low **[unverified]** |
| **TikTok Shop** | Contested | New sellers forced onto TikTok Shop Logistics since 2026-02 | Low-medium |
| **Facebook Marketplace** | Not addressed explicitly | Shipping privileges depend on account standing | Medium-low **[unverified]** |
| **Etsy** | **Prohibited** (except print-on-demand of your own designs) | — | Low |
| **Mercari** | Prohibited (item must be in hand) | — | Low |
| **Poshmark** | Restricted | — | Low |
