## Cross-cutting facts

- **Schedule A design-patent suits (N.D. Illinois):**
  - 127 cases in 2023 and 105 in H1 2024. Defendants won **no** resolved cases from 2023 to mid-2024.
  - Frequent targets: foldable phone holders, wireless charging stands, portable fans, hairbrushes, blender accessories, battery packs.
- **Prop 65:** over 5,000 notices in 2025, mainly lead in glass, ceramics and brass.
- **FCC:** radio-transmitting devices need certification; digital-only devices use SDoC (Supplier's Declaration of Conformity). **UL** warns about counterfeit UL marks on eBay listings.
