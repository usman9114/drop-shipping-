# 01 — eBay dropshipping policy, enforcement, fees

> Researched 2026-11-03 by a web-search agent. The official **US** dropshipping page didn't load, so the policy quotes come from the **UK** help page. Items marked **[unverified]** come from forums or third parties. Check fees and limits in Seller Hub before relying on them.

## 1. Policy

- **Allowed:** "Dropshipping, where you fulfil orders directly from a **wholesale supplier**, is allowed on eBay."
- **Banned:** "listing an item on eBay and then purchasing the item from **another retailer or marketplace**" that ships straight to the buyer.
- AliExpress, Amazon and Walmart aren't named. Read literally, **AliExpress, Amazon, Walmart and Temu are all "another retailer or marketplace"**, so the classic AliExpress→eBay model is **non-compliant**. In practice it's a grey area: enforcement mostly comes through bad metrics and buyer complaints (Amazon boxes, slow delivery), not audits.
- The seller stays responsible for delivery within the stated handling and delivery time, and for buyer satisfaction.
- Sanctions: listing removal, warning, activity restrictions, suspension.

## 2. Seller performance standards (US)

Evaluated on the 20th of each month. The look-back is 3 months if the seller had more than 400 transactions, otherwise 12 months.

| Metric | Above Standard limit | Top Rated |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction defect rate (includes cancellations for out of stock) | ≤ 2% (Below Standard only if more than 4 buyers affected) | ≤ 0.5% (at most 3 buyers) |
| Cases closed without seller resolution | ≤ 0.3% or 2 cases | same |
| Late shipments | — | ≤ 3% or 5 shipments |
| Tracking uploaded within handling time | — | 95% |

- **Fee penalties:** Below Standard adds **+6%** to the final value fee (+7% after 4 or more months). A "Very High" INAD rate adds **+5%** (rising to 6%).
- Forum anecdotes say Amazon-packaged deliveries lead to INAD disputes and then restrictions or suspension. Appeals reportedly rarely succeed **[unverified]**.

## 3. New sellers

- **Selling limits:** caps on item count and value, reviewed monthly and raised automatically as metrics improve. You can request an increase, reportedly once every 30 days **[unverified]**. A store subscription doesn't raise limits **[unverified]**.
- **Payment holds:** new sellers may have funds **held up to 21 days**, generally released within 30 days. Uploading tracking speeds release.
  - ⚠️ **Cash-flow trap:** we pay the supplier on day 0 and get paid weeks later. Working capital is needed.

## 4. Fees (US, official fee page)

| Category | Final value fee (up to $7,500) |
|---|---|
| Most categories | **13.6%** |
| Books, Movies & TV, Music | 15.3% |
| Coins, trading cards, comics | 13.25% |
| Guitars & Basses | 6.7% |
| Handbags | 15% up to $2k, then 9% |
| Jewelry | 15% up to $5k, then 9% |
| Athletic shoes $150 or more | 8% (no per-order fee) |

- **Per-order fee:** $0.30 for orders of $10 or less, **$0.40** for orders over $10.
- **Insertion fees:** 250 free listings per month, then $0.35 each.
- **Stores** **[unverified]**: Starter ~$7.95/mo, Basic ~$27.95, Premium ~$74.95. Stores raise the free-listing allowance.
- **Promoted Listings:**
  - Standard is a percentage of sale, charged on ad-attributed sales. Advanced is cost-per-click.
  - Reportedly a 30-day any-click attribution model since 2026-01-13, and a 5% minimum general ad rate for some sellers **[unverified]**.
  - **Plan for 13.6% + a 2–5% ad rate.**

## 5. Recent changes affecting dropshippers

- **De minimis:**
  - Ended for China/HK on 2025-05-02 and for all countries on 2025-08-29.
  - On 2026-02-20, *Learning Resources v. Trump* held that IEEPA doesn't authorize tariffs. A separate order the same day kept the de minimis suspension.
  - The Court of International Trade upheld the suspension on 2026-08-13; an appeal is pending.
  - The replacement duty rate for small parcels is **unclear** (a 15% Section 122 surcharge and a 10% postal duty are both cited). **Check CBP.**
- **Effect:** China-direct shipments now carry duty, fees and slower postal delivery. **Domestic US wholesale suppliers are largely unaffected.** That is the structural opening.

## 6. Seller sentiment (thin evidence)

- Reddit 2025–26 (mirrors and snippets only): it works "with conditions", needs product testing, and is better in higher-margin niches. Posters warn about inflated income claims.
- Even vendors who sell eBay tools (SaleHoo, ZIK) frame it as **side income, not a full-time business**.
